After a review with the Trilemma Foundation, the team found look-ahead errors that had pushed an early score up to 79.35%. Once they were fixed, the final model beat plain dollar-cost averaging in 63.6% of 3,075 rolling one-year windows between 2016 and 2025, about nine in ten when markets were flat or falling but fewer than four in ten when they rose.
